# NNNN. Short imperative title

Status: proposed | accepted | rejected | deprecated | superseded
Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Deciders: Name, Role
Supersedes: NNNN, NNNN

## Context

Describe the situation that requires a decision. Include forces at play,
constraints, and the specific problem being addressed. Be specific.

## Decision

State the decision in the present tense. Be specific enough that a reader can
tell what was chosen and what was not.

## Consequences

Describe the outcomes of the decision: benefits, drawbacks, follow-up work,
and any new constraints introduced.

## Alternatives

List the options considered and rejected, with the reason each was rejected.

## References

- Documents, links, or prior ADRs that support the decision.
